Havenly Privacy Policy

This page describes the intended privacy approach for Havenly, a portfolio and demonstration property platform. The current website is a front-end concept and does not operate as a production real estate service.

1. Overview

Havenly is designed with privacy and transparency in mind. As the platform develops, information may be collected when users interact with property searches, forms, accounts, inquiries, or future intelligent features.

The current portfolio version is primarily a demonstration experience. Its forms and interactive features are not connected to a production database.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on future functionality, Havenly may collect information that users voluntarily provide.

  • Name and contact information.
  • Email address and phone number.
  • Property preferences and search requirements.
  • Property inquiry information.
  • Account information if user accounts are introduced.
  • Information submitted through future AI interactions.
The current demo version does not have a production account system or live property database.

3. How information may be used

If Havenly becomes a live service, collected information may be used to provide and improve the platform.

  • Help users discover relevant properties.
  • Respond to property inquiries.
  • Improve search and recommendation features.
  • Provide account and customer support services.
  • Improve future Havenly AI functionality.
  • Maintain platform security and reliability.

4. Information sharing

Havenly is designed not to sell personal information as a product. If the platform becomes operational, information may need to be shared with service providers or property partners when necessary to provide a requested service.

Any future sharing practices would be governed by the applicable privacy terms and the functionality available at that time.

5. Data security

Security is intended to be an important part of the Havenly platform. Future production systems should use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect user data.

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security, but reasonable measures should be used to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

A future production version of Havenly may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences, understand usage patterns, improve functionality, and maintain secure sessions.

The current portfolio version does not rely on a production cookie-based account system.

7. Your choices

Where applicable, users may have choices regarding the information they provide and how it is used.

  • Request information about data associated with an account.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where applicable.
  • Manage certain communication preferences.

8. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated as Havenly develops new features, services, technology, and data practices.

When this happens, the updated version should replace the previous version on the website and display a new revision date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or the Havenly concept, you can visit the contact page.

Email: hello@havenly.example
